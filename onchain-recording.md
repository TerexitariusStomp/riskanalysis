# Onchain Recording

Hypercerts and Ethereum Attestation Service (EAS) as well as the Hedera Guardian have emerged as some of the top solutions for impact recording. As appropriate Hypercerts will be created for the impact projects and the EAS will be used to further improve the impact claims.&#x20;
