# Liquidation Discount

The Liquidation Discount operates through a sophisticated Dutch auction system that starts at 1% discount and can scale up to a maximum percentage over a maximum time period (currently 5-day period). This mechanism ensures efficient price discovery while providing opportunities for liquidators to acquire collateral at attractive prices. It continuously increases discount over time. The first acceptable bid wins at the current discount level. Auction ends when collateral is purchased or maximum duration reached.\
