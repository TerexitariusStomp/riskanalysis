# Future Improvements

These are some ideas for future improvements.&#x20;

### Dynamic Liquidation Incentives

Dynamic incentives allow for larger liquidations from non-organizationally affiliated individuals as positions become harder to liquidate or as assets get closer to their maximum debt ceilings.

### Dynamic Adjustments

Monitoring: Market conditions should be continuously monitored. If liquidity or trading volume drops, or if sell pressure increases, the financial risk score of an asset needs to be adjusted. This adjustment should help to further decide the parameters set.&#x20;

## Daily Volatility

Measured of volatility changes of assets. To give a better overall average volatility and adjusting ratios accordingly.&#x20;
