# Liquidation Tip

The Liquidation Tip provides direct compensation to liquidators who execute the liquidation process. This incentive structure ensures reliable liquidator participation during market stress periods when rapid liquidation is most critical. Enables participation from various liquidator types, from individual users to automated bots.

The liquidation tip is sourced from the liquidation penalty collected during the process, ensuring that protocol revenue funds liquidator incentives. This creates a self-sustaining system where the cost of poor position management funds the infrastructure needed to maintain protocol health. 1% of the value of positions liquidated goes towards funding this tip wallet.&#x20;
