# Safety Collateral Ratio

This is the minimum required amount of collateral needed to maintain the vault in good standing. If  a vault's collateral value drops below this threshold, it becomes undercollateralized and is at risk of liquidation. If a vault falls below the safety threshold users cannot withdraw additional collateral or mint more AZUSD, effectively freezing borrowing capacity until additional collateral is deposited or debt is repaid. The safety collateral ratio acts as a safeguard to ensure each borrowing position is sufficiently backed by collateral. For example, a safety collateral ratio of 150% means users must maintain at least $1.50 worth of collateral for every $1 of stablecoin debt. Higher volatility assets require higher collateralization ratios. Stablecoins are an example of an asset that typically is not volatile while Ethereum is an example of an asset that is. Volatility as well as the financial risk assessment helps to determine this ratio.&#x20;
