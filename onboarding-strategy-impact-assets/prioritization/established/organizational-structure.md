# Organizational Structure

Decentralized autonomous organizations receive highest priority consideration due to their role often as stewards of open-source protocols with global applicability. DAOs demonstrate governance transparency, community ownership, and alignment with decentralized finance principles that enhance long-term sustainability prospects. Organizations pursuing gradual decentralization receive intermediate priority, while centralized entities rank lowest in the prioritization hierarchy. This preference reflects the protocol's commitment to supporting genuinely decentralized infrastructure that reduces counterparty risks and enhances censorship resistance.
