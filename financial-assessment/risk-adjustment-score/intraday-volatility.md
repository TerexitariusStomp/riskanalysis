# Intraday Volatility

The expression "high\_24h - low\_24h" refers to the difference between the highest and lowest price of an asset within a 24-hour period. This simple range is a direct measure of short-term price volatility. The closer this value is to 0, the less the asset’s price has fluctuated in that period, indicating lower volatility. Conversely, a larger value signals greater price swings and higher volatility.
