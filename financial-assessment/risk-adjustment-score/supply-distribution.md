# Supply Distribution

Key Definitions

* **Circulating Supply:** The number of tokens currently available for trading in the market—not locked or reserved.
* **Total Supply:** The total number of tokens that have been created, including those locked in smart contracts or reserved, minus any that have been burned.
* **Max Supply:** The absolute maximum number of tokens that can ever exist, as defined by the protocol.

### The Formula: (circulating\_supply - total\_supply - max\_supply)

* **Closer to Zero:**
  * Most tokens are already in the market.
  * Lower risk of price shocks from unlocks.
  * Collateral value is more predictable.
* **More Negative:**
  * Many tokens are yet to be released.
  * Higher risk of future dilution and price drops.
  * Collateral value is less certain; risk management must account for upcoming unlocks or mints.
