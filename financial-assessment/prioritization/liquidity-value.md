# Liquidity Value

High liquidity ensures that the protocol can efficiently liquidate collateral with minimal price slippage, protecting the stability of its stablecoin and minimizing losses during market downturns. It also enables accurate price discovery and reduces the risk of price manipulation.

In contrast, low liquidity increases the risk of failed or costly liquidations, unreliable pricing, and potential protocol insolvency. By prioritizing tokens with deep liquidity we maintain a more resilient, efficient, and trustworthy platform for our users.&#x20;
