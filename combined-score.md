# Combined Score

Once the Financial and Impact assessment has been complete the data from both are loaded into a singular spreadsheet. The ranking for each asset 1 being best and the higher the worse the score. The ranking for the financial and impact score are added together to give a  combined score. This score is the priority at which the asset should be viewed as collateral.&#x20;
